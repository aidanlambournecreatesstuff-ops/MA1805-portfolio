# Movement
In Movement, physical movement is displayed through the shrinking of a square and the translation of the graph. The illusion of movement is also established through the rapid spawn of 'ants.' A lover of ants, I thought it would be fun to show movement through the devouring of a dropped sugar cube in a field of grass. To shrink the sugar cube toward the center, I had to move the graph along with it. Variables also had to be established for the X and Y properties of both the square and the graph to create the movement, and to create bounds in which the ants could randomly spawn.
